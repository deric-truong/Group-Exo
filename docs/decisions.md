| 2026-10-19 | CFTC | Index each report by its release date = next Friday after the measurement day | Avoid look-ahead bias; the old "+3 days" rule gave wrong days in holiday weeks | Deric |
| 2026-10-19 | CFTC | Flag reports measured during the Oct 2013 and Dec 2018–Jan 2019 US government shutdowns (`delayed_release`) and exclude them from event studies | They were published weeks late, so the next-Friday release date is wrong | Deric |
| 2026-11-01 | BIS | Keep the quarter-end date as index and add `release_date` = quarter end + 4 months; use `release_date` when aligning with market data | Avoid look-ahead bias: BIS publishes each quarter months after it ends | Deric |
| 2026-11-01 | BIS | Check units (`UNIT_MEASURE = JPY`, `UNIT_MULT = 6`) and duplicates on download; raise an error if they change | The conversion to trillions of yen relies on them | Deric |
| 2026-11-01 | BIS | Re-fetch rather than keep old files; past quarters are revised (e.g. 2025-Q3 went from 62.7 to 64.0 tn JPY) | BIS revises recent quarters in later releases | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | Yahoo | Set one-day USD/JPY bad prints to NaN (`remove_spikes`): a jump >3% away from both neighbours, in the same direction, while the neighbours stay within 2% of each other. Removes 2008-04-08, 05-08, 10-08, 12-08 | Yahoo errors (e.g. 109.24 between 92.82 and 92.30) create fake extreme returns and bias the tail tests (H1) | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | Yahoo | Apply this rule to USD/JPY only, not to equities, VIX, Bitcoin, oil or USD/MXN | In those series, big moves that reverse the next day are real (e.g. VIX, USD/MXN 2008-10-22 during the peso crisis) | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | Yahoo | Keep weekend rows and do not forward-fill prices in the saved file | Weekend rows come from Bitcoin only; filling gaps would create fake zero returns. Alignment is done in the merge step | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | MoF | Keep the intervention date as index and add `release_date` = quarter end + 45 days; use `release_date` when aligning with market data | Daily details only come out with the quarterly report (e.g. Q3 2024 on 8 Nov 2024); 45 days is on or after every release checked. Not used as index: all days of a quarter share one release date | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | MoF | Keep yen interventions only (drop USD/rupiah 1997–98 and USD/mark 1991), sign amounts + yen bought / − yen sold, convert 100 million yen to billions, and add up same-day operations (USD + EUR) | One signed yen amount per day; the other pairs are not yen interventions | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | MoF | On download, check 9 columns, at least one intervention row, dates in order, and that each quarter's days add up to MoF's quarterly total (gap ≤ 5 ×100m yen); raise an error otherwise | Catches a changed file format or parsing errors; rounding alone gives gaps up to 2 (MoF note 2) | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | MoF | Do not use the monthly totals MoF publishes around month end | They have no daily split; they would reveal an intervention earlier and could be added later if needed | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | FRED | Keep the month-start date as index and add `release_date` = month start + 3 months; use `release_date` when aligning with market data | Avoid look-ahead bias: each value is a monthly average published after the month ends (JP about 2 months later) | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | FRED | Rename columns by series id, not by position; check on download for an empty file, missing or all-NaN series, duplicate or non-month-start dates, and rates outside -2% to 25% | Fail loudly if FRED changes the series; renaming by position could silently swap US and JP | Deric |
| 2026-11-02 | FRED | Keep gaps as NaN: `jp_3m` before 2002-04, `us_3m` 2020-04, the latest month of `jp_3m` | Filling would invent values; alignment is done in the merge step | Deric |
